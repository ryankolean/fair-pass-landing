Brand guide, version 1, Nov 23, 2026. A loud gig-poster look on a black background, built for the Detroit price-gap campaign and everything after it.

01The name

  • Always FAIRPASS: one word, all caps, in the wordmark, in headlines and in captions.
  • Never: Fair Pass, FairPass, Fairpass or FP on its own.
  • Social handle: @fairpasstix, written in lowercase like every handle.

02The slogan

The price on the ticket should be the price of the ticket. It comes from the Detroit campaign plan and is the core message of the brand.

  • Stacked in five lines: THE PRICE / ON THE TICKET / SHOULD BE / THE PRICE / OF THE TICKET.
  • Highlight only "should be": every other word stays ice. Never highlight any other words.
  • Highlight color: magenta on blue-lead posts, electric blue on magenta-lead posts.
  • One line: in a caption or a bio, write it as a normal sentence in sentence case.

  • Electric blue on Night: only at 24 px and up, or bold 19 px and up. Use Blue tint for anything smaller.
  • Ice text on a magenta fill: don't. It's too low in contrast. Put Night text on magenta instead.
  • One accent per element: never fade blue into magenta, and never use both on one word.

07Voice

  • Plain and factual: short sentences, real numbers, no hype.
  • Every number has a source and a time. Recheck resale prices right before posting.
  • Talk about the gap, not anyone's intent. No "scam" or "illegal". Lawsuits only as filed allegations or stated verdicts, with a link.
  • Honest about where we are: say "we're building" and point to the waitlist. Don't promise features the product doesn't have yet.
  • Fan receipts: blur names, emails, order numbers and barcodes. Credit fans only if they agree.

These rules come from the guardrails in the Detroit campaign plan in the repo.

08Do and don't

Do

  • Keep every background Night.
  • Lead with one big number.
  • Alternate blue-lead and magenta-lead posts.
  • Highlight "should be" and nothing else.

Don't

  • Use white, gray or photo backgrounds behind the slogan.
  • Highlight other words in the slogan.
  • Mix both accents in one word or gradient.
  • Set body text in Anton.
